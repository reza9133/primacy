Reading the contract on Studionet

Four steps, one fair line

Nobody can jump the queue, copy a rival's report, or get paid twice for the same defect.

Fund

A sponsor writes the scope and locks the reward. Hunters see that the money is already there.

Commit

A hunter posts only a fingerprint of the finding plus a small bond. That fixes their place in line.

Reveal

Later the hunter publishes the text. It must match the fingerprint exactly, or it is rejected.

Judge and pay

Validators decide if it is in scope and new. Contract code, never the model, moves the money.

Built for the awkward cases

Bug bounties break in predictable places. Primacy was designed around them.

🔒

Commit before you speak

The finding is hidden behind a hash until the hunter reveals it, so no one can read it and rush in with a copy.

🧠

Duplicates by meaning

Rewording does not make a finding new. Validators compare the underlying defect, and a deterministic guard catches near copies.

⚖️

Code moves the money

The model only fills in a small form. Amounts and recipients come from contract rules that anyone can audit.

🛡️

Bonds against spam

Every finding locks a bond. Valid work gets it back. Invalid or abandoned findings pay the sponsor.

🧱

Hardened against tricks

Reports are treated as untrusted data inside a sealed fence, so text like "ignore the rules" is just text.

⏱️

Bounded retries

If validators cannot decide, the call backs off and gives up after a fixed budget. The bond is refunded and the line moves on.
